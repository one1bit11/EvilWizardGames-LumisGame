Lumis by Evil Wizard Games

--------------------

Mango has fallen into the sewers while on her way to a date with her girlfriend! Oh no! Can she use her sticky slime abilities to climb out and reunite with Melon on the surface?

This build contains the full game of Lumis.

--------------------

Controls:

W/A/S/D: Move
Mouse: Look
Space: Jump
Mouse Click: Stick to Surfaces
Shift: Sprint

R: Respawn at Checkpoint
ESC (Hold): Quit to Main Menu

--------------------

External Assets:

Sound Libraries: _CORE 5 Pro Plus, Blastdrive 3.0
Font: Comic Relief by Jeff Davis
Software: Godot, Blender, Procreate, Adobe Substance 3D Painter, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, FL Studio, Adobe Audition
